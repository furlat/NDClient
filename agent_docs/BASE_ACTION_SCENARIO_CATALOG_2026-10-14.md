# Base-action scenario catalog — 14 October 2026

This is the scenario and coverage plan for the Godot Studio base-action work. Its single editable scenario source is [`authoring/studio/scenarios.json`](../authoring/studio/scenarios.json); this Markdown is generated by `python3 tools/render_studio_scenarios.py`. It replaces neither the engine rules nor the [active Studio implementation plan](GODOT_STUDIO_BASE_MECHANICS_PLAN_2026-10-13.md). It names the situations that must be demonstrated, the participants and causal tracks that must be visible, and the evidence still missing. **It does not authorize implementing every listed conditional feature, importing more effects, changing rules, or building another scenario/animation framework.**

The desired browser organization is **action → meaningful interaction → outcome**, for example **Dash → move before and after Dash → allowance increases, gait unchanged**. A seed, journal directory, generic “Basic actions” bucket, or a green compiler result is not an adequate scenario description. Recording filenames belong in the evidence details.

“Complete” here means that every currently registered standard action has a place, together with the requested attacks, reactions, economy, life, blood and ordinary object interactions, and the important interactions between them. It does not mean that all combinations of every spell, creature, item, map and condition have already been recorded or rendered. Conditional engine capabilities and unavailable public action variants are named explicitly rather than silently omitted or fabricated.

### Evidence labels used below

- **Recorded — Rxx:** the cited real journal contains the named outcome. Only that outcome is claimed. Native rendering, audio and variant coverage remain separate receipts.
- **Partial — Rxx:** a related capture exists, but it lacks some required setup, comparison, participant or outcome. It does not pass the named scenario yet.
- **Missing capture:** the engine path exists, but no matching current inventory evidence was found. Capture legally; do not edit dice, facts, HP or conditions into a journal.
- **Source/renderer gap:** an authored requirement, missing assignment, missing source or missing consumer is named. A condition badge cannot satisfy a movement trail, and a child's effect cannot satisfy its parent's action body.
- **Conditional / availability unverified:** engine support or an authoring recipe exists, but this exact player variant/setup is not established in current public choices. Verify discovery first. Do not introduce a new command merely to make the scenario run.

For stochastic cases, the expected outcome means the result that must be **observed and retained** for that scenario. An attack may legitimately miss while searching for a hit recording. Keep that miss as a separate case; do not call it a failed renderer or rewrite it into a hit.

## 3. Shared presentation contract for every scenario

The table entries below name the scenario-specific tracks. These common requirements apply to every row:

1. **Actor and participant ownership.** The initiator owns its action body. Each reactor owns its own reaction body. Each actual damage recipient owns its hit/material/number/life/blood tracks. The moving actor keeps its interrupted trajectory and resumes only if the received sequence permits it. Object changes belong to the actual object UUID. A shared parent is causal context, not permission to duplicate bodies for every child.
2. **All recipients respond.** Every nonterminal hit eligible for the authored `TakeDamage` body must show it, including a second actor, a reactor hit by retaliation, and repeated hits on the same actor. Preserve the existing terminal/lethal exception: do not force `TakeDamage` before a lethal fall or replay a fall on an already Dying/Dead body. Show the exact flash/HP/number/life clocks and source reason for the exception.
3. **Commit order remains authoritative.** Bodies and effect anchors may schedule presentation; they cannot reorder server facts, display final HP early, clear a condition locally, spend resources speculatively, or continue a canceled step. Show parent/child event UUIDs and state markers in the inspector.
4. **Source layers remain distinct.** Body, equipment, hands, native/registered effects, material filters, projectile, feedback and audio are independent authored contributions. A positive-duration body is required only where the effective recipe enables one. Missing assignments/consumers stay visible as named expected lanes.
5. **Audio is causal and licensed.** Use selected sourced cues, at the actual body release/contact/application/landing/removal marker. Do not play a hit cue on a miss, a landing cue for an uncommitted landing, or replay history when scrubbing. Missing cues remain named; a placeholder beep is not coverage. Native playback proof and artistic mix approval are separate.
6. **Same sampler in Studio and player.** Forward playback, pause, reverse seek, direct seek, loop and source reload use the same prepared state and production renderer. No scene-specific scheduler, event injection, miniature renderer or per-scenario outcome logic.
7. **Useful bounded viewing interval.** Include a short genuine initial idle, the operation and its useful aftermath. Current Studio lead-in/inter-operation holds are presentation-only; group-local commit order is unchanged. End after the last relevant visible/audio effect plus a short inspectable rest. Do not append an unseen other-faction duration, empty effect padding or an invented ground stain. Do not trim a real reaction, source tail, removal, death save or sound to make the loop shorter.
8. **Perspective is part of evidence.** Record and replay each relevant faction independently. An actor may remain Hidden but be detected by an observer. A local source light is not permission to disclose a concealed actor/item identity. No unseen placeholder rows or guessed future states.

For each captured scenario retain the exact action choices and selected option, command/operation prefix, participant UUIDs, key event/condition/object UUIDs, resource before/after values, outcome, source pointers and the actual semantic/presentation boundaries. These are ordinary descriptor facts, not a new scenario language or a checksum framework.

## 20. Framework acceptance and subsequent study order

### Per-scenario acceptance record

Each scenario needs an evidence row with **setup verified → legal choice/preview selected → outcome witnessed → complete event/state parity → source/lane coverage → native forward/seek proof → audio proof**, marking stages unexercised when not run. This is a report checklist, not a new runtime state machine. Do not reduce it to one green boolean.

The decisive checks are:

- **Real intended outcome:** exact participants, target/position, costs, rolls, contacts, condition UUIDs, object UUIDs and final state. A missed/intercepted action is its own case, not proof of a hit/success.
- **All participants:** for every received damage occurrence, inspect victim body/exception, material/number/HP/life/blood and source timing. Include attackers who themselves take damage and repeated recipients.
- **Allowance versus rate:** retain actual engine speed and movement spending alongside source FPS/playback rate/duration. Compare matched routes; do not infer “faster Dash” from how far it can travel.
- **No empty long tails:** inspect useful end time in the selected audience. Preserve real effect/audio/death-save tails; remove only empty padding/unseen-placeholder time from the preview loop. Current case intervals must derive from the compiled index, not stale hardcoded timings.
- **Normative lanes:** show expected but unimplemented authored tracks and exact pointers, including absent authoring assignments. Distinguish unassigned, referenced source missing, native conversion not selected, renderer missing, intentionally disabled and unexercised. No blanket “implemented” because a sibling component exists.
- **Causal audio:** one-shot count and actual crossing; silence on historical scrub; active-loop restoration where authored; selected source/license; no cue created from a refused or undisclosed action. Per-cue PCM is not an arbitrary simultaneous-mix guarantee.
- **Real sampling:** forward from before the action, direct/reverse seek through release/contact/application/removal, pause, replay/loop, and reload rejection preserving the old admitted view. Native proof must assert the reached clock/state rather than merely wait for a timeout.
- **Camera and support:** four quarters and representative zooms for geometry-sensitive cases; actual source body/gear/hand/VFX, transparent material, floor support and corpse placement. Do not multiply all nongeometric semantic tests by every camera.
- **Privacy/authority:** separate faction captures; no future canonical state, local prediction, invented targets, HP, conditions, residues or source identities. Source-owned light calibration remains native; it is not a new client stealth system.
