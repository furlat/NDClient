## Why wider families are not the next ready cohort

- **Vampiric Touch healing:** the existing original baseline and durable
  `scenarios/vampiric-touch` contain actual HealFact73 with `actual_healing=0`.
  The source's `healing_admitted` requires positive actual healing. This is a
  genuine negative control; a wounded-caster positive recording is still needed.
  Its held hand lifecycle already exists, while repeat-action attribution and
  exact activation/heal join need separate work. Do not fabricate a heal from
  half the damage or make the first-hit cohort claim the rest.
- **Glyph and Dragon's Breath:** exact spatial/current state and some constructor
  identity are visible, but grant element/action discovery and hidden payload or
  trigger provenance are not broadly supplied. The Glyph original even admits
  and retires its zone within packet8; that is not a long idle armed fixture.
  These need a deliberately bounded owner/phase study before a shared binder
  is claimed. Review dictionaries alone are not such evidence.
- **Hex/Hunter's Mark, smites and class riders:** actual quarry/condition members
  do not by themselves establish all source sustain/child/rider links. The
  current Divine Smite baseline exposes an attack with Slashing+Radiant but
  only one applied DamageFact typed Slashing; its blood secondary type is not
  an independently attributed Radiant rider. Likewise the existing passive
  class studies retain missing effective-handler attribution. Broadly reusing
  the generic positive-damage packet would turn unrelated damage into a rider.
- **Selection aliases:** dragon resistance/breath and paladin aura selectors
  remain the exact missing public operands documented in
  `CONDITION_SELECTOR_PRODUCER_GAPS_2026-10-20.md`.

Deferred actor-history shaders, Counterspell's unavailable actual moving
charge/trajectory contract, rejected artwork and the new weapon package are
outside this read-only recommendation. Source presence, original scenario,
actual native framebuffer, timing/performance and user acceptance remain
independent facts for both recommended cohorts.
