## Event-state details worth addressing once

- `event_binding` and `event_state` currently have no client producer. The
  delivered `conditions/runtime.gd` already enforces independent owner/source
  binding, monotonic observation revision, event-serial deduplication, scoped
  retirement and phase/owner ages. Use the actual native numeric identities as
  strings where its contract asks for serials; keep occurrence and lineage
  separately. Stream generation changes on replay reset, not on each event.
- Tenacity's Factory gate requires a committed positive Bludgeoning result,
  Miss/Crit Miss, exact action/lineage/victim, `range_type=Reach`, contact subset
  and matching `contact_surface_revision`. `AttackFact` does not expose range
  type. `DamageResultFact.source_condition_uuid` is optional; the frozen
  `MyrmidonTenacity.miss` calls `receive_damage` without explicitly supplying
  it. Inspect actual effective-handler/emitted-lineage evidence; do not assume
  that optional field will identify Tenacity on every record.
- `NativePersistent.lifecycle_samples` normally targets the condition host.
  Tenacity's accent must instead target the actual damaged actor while retaining
  its source condition owner. Passing its victim UUID only inside `event_state`
  leaves the generic geometry producer attached to the wrong actor.
- Current `EntitySurface`/`NativeInputs` do not publish the required
  `region_id=damage_contact` / `mouth` and matching region revision. A complete
  body/head AABB is not the declared region. Resolve a measured subset from the
  authored actual contact/mouth socket and present it through the existing
  optical surface lane; absent authored sockets stay explicit.
- The 13 breath-readiness variants require `issued`, `free_action_uuid`,
  `granted_lineage` and `breath_type`. These are absent from public
  `ConditionState`. Seeing Multiattack or a free action in choices is not proof
  of this exact allowance/owner/lifetime. Preserve those named gaps.
- Four elemental variants require factual `qualified_bonus_observed`, exact
  damage-packet identities and a `prerequisite` selector; three Myrmidon variants
  require factual `strike_dice_observed`, configured action/type and an exact
  weapon/item. The schema lacks those condition selectors and has no public
  damage-roll packet Fact. Actual effective-handler attribution can establish
  that a handler modified/emitted an occurrence, but does not automatically
  identify one rider component inside total damage. Do not reconstruct dice.
- Counterspell is a useful later small selector: `ActionFact.reaction` already
  contains `triggered_lineage`, `succeeded`, `automatic`. Its source study also
  needs exact incoming source-prefix/contact geometry and actual constructor
  identities. `reaction_observation` alone cannot legitimize its fixture path.

audit counted only the five stored in `abilities.actions`. Nine additional
