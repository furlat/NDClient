## Existing ownership seam

`EntitySurface.sample` calculates the named optical support centroid inside its
existing point-assembly loop and retains it in the same cached optical packet.
There is no extra image scan, readback, measurement job or source-art change.

The existing NativeInputs owner resolves the exact item and optional grip once.
NativeRenderer passes that registration and the same surface packet into
SpellPlacement and sampling. An actual authored hand keeps the original point,
binding and placement tuple. A qualified source with a genuinely absent grip may
use the named optical anchor as its receiving frame; `binding.hand` and the
placement packet's `hand` remain absent. The source retains its own optional
orientation default. This is not an inferred hilt or an offhand alias.

Fallback is limited to exact missing-authorship reasons. A known grip whose
actual source images are still being prepared keeps its preparation owner; it
never temporarily moves onto the centroid. Missing geometry remains preparation
work or an explicit unavailable item.
Explicit authored hand endpoints, ambiguous/missing item identities, legacy
source obligations, Flame Blade lifetime checks, and event-gated outcomes are
not bypassed. Source setters and clocks retain their existing owners.
